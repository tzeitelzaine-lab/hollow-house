# Host guide — The Heiress of Hollow House

> **Contains the full solution.** Keep this document and the host dashboard off any shared screen.

## Before the event (the day before)

1. Complete the README setup and create your event.
2. Play the case once in **preview mode** (host dashboard → Open preview mode). The walkthrough below lets you do this in about 10 minutes.
3. Decide on team sizes (3–6) and choose a captain for each team.
4. Prepare your welcome message, using the template below.

## Welcome message template

> Welcome to **The Heiress of Hollow House**, a HighCall VA Multiverse mystery!
> Join here: **https://your-site/?code=YOURCODE**
> Each team's captain registers the team, chooses a 4–8 digit PIN, and **shares their screen** with teammates on the call. Talk through every decision together.
> Hints cost 2 minutes. A wrong final accusation costs 3. Ordinary wrong answers are free.
> Fastest fair solution becomes the **HighCall VA Multiverse Champions**.

## Run sheet (60–75 minutes)

| Time | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Welcome everyone. Send breakout rooms to their teams with the join link. |
| 0:05 | Captains register and read the briefing. Each team starts its own clock with **Start investigation**. |
| 0:10–0:55 | Watch the dashboard: stage, hints and last activity. Visit any team that has been idle for more than 8 minutes. |
| ~0:50 | Close registration if you have not already. |
| 0:55 | Give a 5-minute warning to teams still playing. |
| 1:00 | Bring everyone back. Show the leaderboard (`#/leaderboard/YOURCODE`), never the dashboard. Celebrate the champions. |
| 1:05 | Optional: ask the winning team to explain the case in their own words. |
| After | Export results to CSV from the dashboard. |

**If something breaks for everyone** (a platform outage or a call drop), press **Pause event**. Every team's clock freezes exactly, and teams cannot act until you press **Resume**.

**If one team's laptop dies**, a teammate opens the join link, chooses **Resume our investigation**, and enters the same team name and PIN. The timer and penalties carry over.

## The solution

**Adrian Cross**, Evelyn's fiancé, killed her with the **brass candlestick** from the apartment. He did it to stop her exposing his **fraud against the Vale Housing Trust**: $112,300 paid to Larkspur Property Services, a shell company registered at his own office, for repairs that were never done.

### The fixed timeline

| Time | Event | Proven by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8:10 PM | Noah arrives | Entrance camera; Noah admits it |
| 8:22 PM | Party photo A captured (Adrian with both cufflinks) | Photo A metadata |
| 8:25 PM | Noah leaves after an argument | Entrance camera |
| 8:32 PM | Noah pays at Ortiz Corner Market and stays on camera until 9:14 | Receipt and footage |
| 8:40 PM | Evelyn leaves Tessa a voice message: Noah has gone and she is fine | Voicemail and phone records |

| 9:26 PM | Photo B: Adrian at the podium, left cufflink missing | Event photographer, verified against the livestream |
| 9:35 PM | Marta finds Evelyn and calls 911 | Marta's statement |

### The four evidence chains

- **Motive.** The trust payment records, Evelyn's resident notes, the vendor registry (Suite 1104 matches Adrian's business card) and the auditor email (meeting at 9:00 the next morning).
- **Presence.** The entrance camera (8:47–9:08) and the 8:51 text. The caretaker confirms the rear door is chained, so the camera covers the only way in.
- **Alibi failure.** Photo A was *captured* at 8:22 and only *uploaded* at 9:14. Photo B at 9:26 comes after he left. The time of death falls inside his visit.
- **Scene.** The dented candlestick base matches the injury. The window glass fell outward and the cash was left behind, so the burglary was staged. The cufflink is engraved "E & A", and Evelyn's one-of-a-kind pair was established earlier in both Adrian's and Tessa's profiles. Photo B shows him wearing only one. The cufflink proves something *only* together with the camera log.

### Red herrings and their explanations

| Lead | Why it is not the answer |
| --- | --- |
| Noah's angry text and visit | He left at 8:25. Evelyn was alive at 8:40. Market footage covers 8:31–9:14. His secret was selling candid photos. |
| Leo's "it was the photographer" | An assumption. His revised statement describes formal shoes and a black overcoat, not denim. |
| Celeste's letter about the audit | She feared scandal and losing her trustee seat. Henrietta Lowe places her at the auction table all evening, and she approved none of the Larkspur payments. |
| Marta's master key and secrecy | Evelyn asked her to keep the secret. Marta's own secret (reduced rent for the family in 2A) is unrelated. |
| Tessa withholding messages | She was keeping her promise to Evelyn and handed everything over the next morning. |
| $5 million life insurance | Real, but it had existed since the engagement. The trigger for that night was the 9:00 AM auditor meeting. |

## Stage-by-stage answers

| Stage | Required answers |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Secure the scene | Inspect bag, lease, candlestick, window and notepad (the cufflink is optional). Alias: **Eva Reed**. Staged burglary: **glass fell outside** + **wallet cash untouched**. Weapon: **brass candlestick**. |
| 2. Meet the suspects | Open all six profiles. Confirmed: Noah went to 4B; Marta knew; Tessa knew; Celeste at the masquerade. Unverified: Adrian at the masquerade all evening; the man Leo saw was Noah. |
| 3. Follow the money | Purpose: **meet residents and compare their accounts with trust records**. Payee: **Larkspur Property Services**. Connected: **Adrian Cross**. |
| 4. Reconstruct the night | Timeline in the order of the table above (12 cards, 8:10 → 9:35). Failing alibi: **Adrian**, because photo A was **captured before** he entered. Contradictions: "never left" → **entrance camera log**; Leo "it was Noah" → **market receipt and footage** (or **Leo's second statement**); "photo shows 9:14" → **masquerade photographs**. |
| 5. Connect the evidence | Suspect **Adrian**. Motive pin: trust payments, resident notes, vendor registry or auditor correspondence. Opportunity pin: entrance camera log or the text to the auditor. Physical pin: **gold cufflink**. Deductions: capture before visit / matches his pair and one is missing at 9:26 / burglary staged. |
| 6. Accusation | **Adrian Cross**; motive **to stop Evelyn exposing fraud against the housing trust**; weapon **brass candlestick**; any three of: trust payment records, resident notes, vendor registry, auditor correspondence, entrance camera log, text to the auditor, masquerade photographs, medical summary, gold cufflink, Leo's second statement. |

## Helping stuck teams (free nudges, no penalty)

| Where | Nudge |
| --- | --- |
| Stage 1 | "What would a real burglar have taken?" |
| Stage 2 | "Who says something that makes *themselves* look worse?" |
| Stage 3 | "Put the payment list next to what the residents told Evelyn." |
| Stage 4 (most common) | "Photo A has two different times. What does each one measure?" |
| Stage 5 | "Does your physical evidence point to one specific person? Why?" |
| Stage 6 | "Why did the killer go there *that particular night*?" |

Wrong accusations tell teams how many of the four parts hold up, but not which parts.
